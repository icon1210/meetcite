# User study protocol (RQ3, NFR4)

**Do not start until the university's ethics committee has approved the study.**
The documents submitted with the ethics application are in `docs/ethics/` (Participant Information Sheet,
Consent Form, Study Protocol and Questionnaire, and the text answers for the form).

## Design in brief
* **Part 1, accuracy (no participants):** AMI Meeting Corpus recordings (public, CC BY 4.0) processed in C1 and C2,
  labelled blind by the research team; a second member labels a sample for agreement (Cohen's kappa).
* **Part 2, usability:** 5–10 adult UWS students or staff review **one pre-processed AMI meeting** in MeetCite
  for about 30 minutes. **No participant is recorded and no participant meeting is used**; the session itself is
  not recorded. Nobody the researchers teach, assess or supervise is recruited. No payment.

## Materials
* MeetCite (live site or local), one test account per participant created by the researchers and named only by a
  participant code (P01, P02…). The AMI meeting is already processed in each account.
* Participant Information Sheet, Consent Form, task sheet and questionnaire (`questionnaire.md`).

## Procedure
1. Send the information sheet at least 24 hours before; on the day, answer questions and take written consent.
2. Tasks (researcher notes difficulties, helps only if the participant is stuck for two minutes):
   1. Find out what was decided about [topic].
   2. Find the action items assigned to [person] and mark one as done.
   3. Check whether one summary point is correct by using its source link.
   4. Rename "Speaker 1" to the name given in the meeting.
   5. Export the minutes as a PDF.
3. Questionnaire: SUS, three ratings and two comments.
4. Thank the participant and remind them they can withdraw until [date].

## Data handling (UK GDPR)
* Participant codes only on questionnaires and notes; names appear only on consent forms, stored separately.
* All study data in UWS Microsoft 365, accessible to the team and supervisor only.
* Test accounts contain no personal data and are deleted at the end of the study.
* Cloud AI services (AssemblyAI, Anthropic) process only AMI material in this study, never participant data.
* Anonymised data deleted once dissertation results are confirmed; consent forms kept per UWS requirements.
